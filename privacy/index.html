This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What data is collected

  • Contact form. Your name, work email address, company and message, plus the page from which you sent it.
  • Email. If you write by email, the address and content of your message.
  • Server logs. The hosting provider records technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type.
  • Fonts. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Why it is used

Your data is used to answer your enquiry and take the steps you request before a possible engagement, and to operate and secure this website. Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and the legitimate interest in running and protecting the website and replying to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your data is not sold and is not used for automated decisions or profiling.

Who receives it

The following service providers process data on behalf of the controller:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages by email.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Microsoft, which provides the email service.
  • Google, which serves the fonts.

Some of these providers process data outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where a country does not offer an adequate level of data protection, the transfer relies on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses or a recognised data privacy framework.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any business relationship that follows, and are then deleted unless the law requires them to be kept longer.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, for it to be corrected or deleted, for its processing to be restricted, or for a copy in a portable format, and you can object to its processing. To do so, write to javierpuga@outlook.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, the authority of the country where you live.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or the law changes. The current version is always published on this page.

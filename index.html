Approach

Three stages, each ending in a decision you can defend to your board.

01

Explore

A market read built on evidence. Which countries, segments and accounts fit, who already owns the relationship, what regulation and procurement demand, and which partners can carry you in.

You leave with: a go or no-go recommendation, a target account list and an entry plan.

02

Open

We represent you in the market. Positioning and materials adapted for local buyers, account-based outreach to named institutions, partner introductions, and first meetings attended with your team.

You leave with: qualified opportunities and an honest view of what buyers said.

03

Build

Once pipeline justifies it, we design the permanent set-up. The coverage model, the first sales and business development hires, the partner agreements, and a playbook your team can run alone.

You leave with: a local operation that stands on its own.

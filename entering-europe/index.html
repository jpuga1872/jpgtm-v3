Why a local EU partner

Buyers who decide differently

Enterprise decisions in Europe are slower, more collective and more risk-averse than most vendors from abroad expect. We know which stakeholders matter and how to pace the sale.

An ecosystem that holds the door

Core platforms, systems integrators and consultancies influence most enterprise purchases. We bring the relationships and know which partners are worth your time.

Regulation that enters the sale early

GDPR, DORA, NIS2 and the AI Act turn into procurement questionnaires long before a contract. We prepare your answers before the buyer asks.

Choosing where to land

Presence without an entity

Your options

Three familiar ways to open Europe, and a fourth.

Hire a country manager

A full executive salary, a recruitment cycle and a legal entity, all committed before you know whether the market responds.

Sign a generalist distributor

Quick to announce, slow to produce. Your product becomes one line in a long portfolio, sold by people who never studied its fit with the market.

Sell from headquarters

No extra cost and little traction. Regulated buyers expect a vendor who knows their rules and someone they can meet.

A fractional growth team on the ground

A senior lead and the specialists your case needs, for as much time as the work requires. A defined scope, a clear decision point and nothing to unwind if the answer is no.

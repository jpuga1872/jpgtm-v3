Why a local partner in Spain

Six reasons a European vendor still needs someone on the ground.

Relationships come first

Enterprise buying in Spain rests on trust built in person. Cold outreach from abroad, in English, rarely earns a meeting.

Business is done in Spanish

Senior decision makers expect proposals, demos and contracts in their language. We work in native Spanish.

Integrators sit between you and the buyer

Large consultancies and systems integrators run much of the technology spend in enterprises and the public sector. Choosing and activating the right one is half the entry.

The public sector has its own rules

Tenders follow the public procurement law, suppliers to the administration are expected to meet the National Security Framework (ENS), and regional governments buy separately.

A concentrated market

Banking, insurance, telecoms and utilities are led by a small number of large groups. Access depends on knowing who decides inside each one.

A bridge to Latin America

Many Spanish groups operate across Latin America. A reference in Spain often opens the door to the region.
